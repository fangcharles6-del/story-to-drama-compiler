- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-30
- Depends on: SDC-ADR-039 / Deterministic Visual Prompt Profiles
- Projection dependency: SDC-ADR-040 / Visual Prompt Profiles Phase 1 Projection Manifest
- Compiler-boundary dependency: SDC-ADR-041 / Visual Prompt Profiles Compiler Integration
- Reference-Prompt dependency: SDC-ADR-042 / Character and Scene Reference Prompt Compiler Input
  Boundary
- Candidate/Qualification dependency: SDC-ADR-043 / Generated Reference Candidate Provenance and
  Qualification Boundary
- Rights/current-status dependency: SDC-ADR-044 / Generated Reference Rights Manifest and
  Current-Status Evidence Boundary
- Baseline: `c95d0e69c4e3ad9d426d64a7a6214682f783c519`
- Authority: `HUMAN_GATE / NOT_AUTHORIZED`
- Data boundary: one complete explicitly supplied generated-reference closure, exact local PNG
  bytes, exact caller-supplied primary Bible/AssetVersion snapshots and privacy-minimized retained Promotion
  role records; first-party synthetic review data only
- Network/spend boundary: zero network calls, zero credential reads, zero Provider requests, zero
  authorized Attempts and zero authorized cost

## Context

SDC now has separate accepted and implemented boundaries for:

- deterministic first-party Visual Prompt Profiles and Prompt receipts;
- deterministic reference Prompt compilation against one existing active primary AssetVersion;
- one exact immutable generated-reference Provider Attempt Outcome and Candidate occurrence;
- generated-reference Candidate Qualification;
- a generated-specific Rights Manifest; and
- deterministic generated current-status evidence, replay and historical as-of receipts.

Those boundaries deliberately stop before promotion. Every ADR-043 and ADR-044 formal document
retains `eligible_for_asset_promotion=false`, and even an exact `CURRENT` historical Receipt grants
no promotion, Provider, Runtime or execution authority.

Current `CharacterAssetVersion` and `SceneAssetVersion` cannot truthfully represent generated
media. Their only provenance literal is `IMPORTED_APPROVED_MEDIA`, and that literal participates in
their deterministic ID projections. Human review, download, copying or re-import cannot transform
the origin of generated bytes. Existing Bibles and Compiler products also bind their exact active
AssetVersion identities, so silently replacing an active binding would change released compiled
artifact bytes and identities.

ADR-039 selected one active primary AssetVersion plus a typed sidecar as the default future design
direction. ADR-043 and ADR-044 left the choice between a generated AssetVersion/Bible V2 family and
that sidecar to a separate decision. This Accepted ADR resolves only that missing decision. It does
not design active generated assets, multi-reference role binding, Provider input or execution.

## Decision summary

This Accepted decision constrains any separately approved BUILD to:

1. choose an append-only generated eligible-asset sidecar as the only V1 promotion
   representation;
2. permanently exclude current `CharacterAssetVersion`, `SceneAssetVersion`, Character/Scene
   Bibles and active bindings from mutation or reinterpretation;
3. add, only after separate BUILD authorization, exactly three top-level formal
   Contracts: Promotion Request, Promotion Decision and Eligible-Asset Sidecar;
4. require an exact Promotion Maker/Checker review over one complete explicitly supplied
   ADR-042/043/044 closure;
5. require complete fresh current-status joint replay twice: once when preparing the Request and
   again at the exact final `promotion_at`;
6. make `promotion_at` equal the final Receipt `as_of`, Promotion Checker review time and Decision
   time;
7. bind the exact active imported primary AssetVersion in each caller-supplied Bible snapshot through a closed full-digest
   primary-binding projection without replacing it;
8. copy the Manifest's exact reviewed Rights scope without widening, narrowing, extending or
   reinterpreting it;
9. make one positive Decision and its Sidecar deterministic, append-only historical evidence;
10. keep later adverse evidence, renewed Qualification/Manifest review and future role binding as
    new append-only closures rather than mutations of an old Sidecar; and
11. preserve complete zero Provider, Runtime, network, credential, cost, Retry, publication,
    retention and training authority.

Any conforming V1 output would mean only:

> One exact generated Candidate occurrence passed the separately scoped Promotion review at one
> explicit historical `promotion_at` and was recorded as a supplemental eligible-asset sidecar
> bound to one unchanged primary AssetVersion.

It would not mean that the generated media became a current V1 AssetVersion, an active Bible
binding, a Provider input, a role-specific reference, a current legal entitlement or an executable
asset.

## Acceptance record and implementation gate

This ADR records acceptance of these design choices:

1. **Representation**: V1 chooses a typed eligible-asset sidecar and rejects both current
   AssetVersion reuse and a generated AssetVersion/Bible V2 family in this boundary.
2. **Model family**: V1 uses exactly three top-level models. It does not add formal Instruction,
   Evidence Record or Promotion Receipt models.
3. **Human review**: one Promotion Maker prepares the exact Request packet; one independent
   Promotion Checker records the Decision.
4. **Role separation**: the Promotion Checker differs from the Promotion Maker, ADR-043
   Qualification Qualifier, ADR-044 Manifest Checker, request-time ADR-044 Status Checker and
   final ADR-044 Status Checker.
5. **Time**: Request and Decision admission use explicit caller-supplied UTC seconds. No ambient
   clock, grace period, automatic renewal or deadline extension exists.
6. **Freshness**: Receipt parsing is never replay. Request preparation and Decision finalization
   each rebuild the complete supplied current-status closure.
7. **Primary binding**: the Sidecar supplements one exact active imported AssetVersion and never
   replaces or mutates it.
8. **Rights scope**: Promotion copies the exact Manifest-reviewed scope. It creates no new Rights
   decision and grants no asset-use authority.
9. **Single media**: one Candidate remains one unsplit composite PNG occurrence. No role is inferred
   from Profile recipes, Prompt constraints or QC expectations.
10. **Historical result**: the Sidecar would record one past decision. Every later consumer would
    have to perform its own fresh status evaluation at its own explicit `as_of`.
11. **Compatibility**: the first 83 Registry entries, all 83 committed Schema bytes, all 16 frozen
    fixture paths and all released Compiler artifacts remain unchanged.
12. **Separate BUILD**: this ADR's Accepted status does not authorize Contract, Schema, fixture,
    codegen or implementation changes.

Acceptance records reviewer approval of the three-model choice, exact fields, role matrix, policy
projection, validity rules, identity DAG, error behavior and downstream isolation in this document.

## Frozen upstream compatibility boundary

This Accepted ADR does not narrow-supersede ADR-039, ADR-043 or ADR-044. Their accepted boundaries
remain authoritative, including:

- generated bytes never validate as current `CharacterAssetVersion` or `SceneAssetVersion`;
- `IMPORTED_APPROVED_MEDIA` never gains a generated-media alternative in existing models;
- existing AssetVersion, Bible, `CharacterAssetBinding`, Storyboard, NIR, PIR, Compilation and Job
  identities remain unchanged;
- ADR-044's one-way DAG remains `Manifest -> Status Subject -> Record -> Receipt`; Manifest never
  embeds a later status value;
- structurally invalid, incomplete, cyclic or non-ancestor-closed status inputs fail without a
  status result;
- only structurally valid evidence may reduce to `INDETERMINATE` under the accepted resolver;
- all existing `eligible_for_asset_promotion=false` values remain exact false values; and
- the accepted dependency order remains:

```text
Candidate / Qualification
  -> Generated Rights Manifest / current status
  -> Promotion
  -> multi-reference role binding / Provider input
  -> Runtime / Provider execution
```

No existing Contract would gain an optional field, union member, new enum literal or duck-typed
compatibility path.

## Terminology and lifecycle

This Accepted decision uses these terms narrowly:

1. **Primary AssetVersion**: the exact imported-media `CharacterAssetVersion` or
   `SceneAssetVersion` that one caller-supplied Bible snapshot marks active and that the ADR-042
   Artifact and Candidate already bind.
2. **Primary binding**: the exact subject, purpose and active AssetVersion closure reconstructed
   from one exact supplied Bible and its exact active AssetVersion.
3. **Promotion Request**: one immutable packet prepared for human review over one exact Candidate,
   Rights, status and primary-binding closure.
4. **Promotion Maker**: the retained semantic identity that prepares the Request packet. The term
   does not authenticate a real person or organizational authority.
5. **Promotion Checker**: the distinct retained semantic identity that records the final scoped
   decision. The term does not authenticate a real person or legal authority.
6. **Promotion Decision**: one immutable positive, negative or indeterminate human decision over
   one exact Request and a fresh status replay at `promotion_at`.
7. **Eligible-Asset Sidecar**: one immutable post-promotion historical value created only from a
   positive Decision. It supplements but never becomes or replaces the primary AssetVersion.
8. **Promotion evidence horizon**: the earliest upstream exclusive deadline copied into a Sidecar.
   It describes the historical evidence window and never asserts present currentness.
9. **Role-binding review**: a later, separate human and Contract boundary that may assign one
   post-promotion media value to a bounded reference role. This Accepted decision does not define it.

The frozen V1 lifecycle is:

```text
CAPTURED_UNQUALIFIED Candidate
  -> positive unexpired Qualification Decision
  -> generated Rights Manifest
  -> generated current-status Record and Request-time CURRENT Receipt
  -> Promotion Request
  -> fresh CURRENT replay at promotion_at
  -> Promotion Decision
       -> REJECT / INDETERMINATE: Decision only
       -> APPROVE: Decision + Eligible-Asset Sidecar
  -> separate future role-binding review
```

No state above mutates an earlier document.

## Promotion representation choice

This Accepted ADR chooses the ADR-039 typed-sidecar direction for V1.

The Sidecar would be a distinct generated-reference type. It would not:

- inherit from, validate as, serialize as or use a discriminated union with current AssetVersion;
- be inserted into `CharacterBible.asset_versions` or `SceneBible.asset_versions`;
- change any Bible `active_asset_version_id`;
- validate as `CharacterAssetBinding`, `InputMaterial` or `ProviderRequest`;
- change existing Compiler inputs or products; or
- create a generated AssetVersion/Bible V2 family.

If SDC later needs generated media to become an active Compiler asset, a separate versioned ADR
would have to define truthful generated AssetVersion/Bible V2 types and a versioned Compiler
integration.
That later work cannot modify the V1 types or be inferred from this Sidecar.

## Acyclic Promotion identity DAG

The frozen identity graph is one-way:

```text
ADR-042 Artifact
  -> ADR-043 Outcome -> Candidate -> Qualification Request -> Qualification Decision
  -> ADR-044 Manifest -> Status Subject -> Request/Instruction/Decision -> Record -> Receipt

exact upstream closure + exact primary binding + exact Manifest-reviewed Rights scope
  -> Promotion review payload
  -> Promotion Maker action
  -> Promotion Request
  -> fresh promotion_at status Receipt + Promotion Checker action
  -> Promotion Decision
  -> Eligible-Asset Sidecar
```

The Promotion review payload would not contain Maker or Checker action digests. The Maker action
would bind that payload. The Request would bind the payload and Maker action. The Checker action
would bind the exact Request, final fresh Receipt, final supplied primary-binding digest, gate tuple,
issue tuple, basis and disposition, but never a Decision or Sidecar ID/SHA. The Decision would bind
the Checker action. The Sidecar would bind the Decision.

The Decision would not contain a Sidecar ID/SHA. No placeholder digest, empty digest, reverse field,
post-construction mutation or second-pass identity rewrite is allowed.

## Exact atomic supplied closure

Request preparation and Decision finalization would accept explicit exact values and bytes in two
ordered phases. Neither phase would discover a current, latest or best value from a store.

Both phases would revalidate this common upstream closure:

- the exact ADR-042 Reference Prompt Artifact, Profile Snapshot, render input, Prompt bytes and
  PromptRenderReceipt closure required by its released verifier;
- the exact ADR-043 successful Provider Attempt Outcome;
- the exact Candidate and raw PNG bytes for output ordinal zero;
- the exact Qualification Request and positive Decision;
- all ten retained Qualification evidence documents;
- exact Qualification preparer and qualifier identity/action records;
- the exact ADR-044 generated Rights Manifest;
- all nine Manifest review evidence documents;
- exact Manifest Maker and Checker identity/action records.

Request preparation would additionally receive only:

- the exact request-time generated current-status Subject, Request, Instruction, Decision, Evidence
  Record and historical Receipt;
- every request-time target Source Observation, complete predecessor/ancestor chain and
  reconciliation branch required by ADR-044;
- exact request-time Status Preparer and Checker identity/action records;
- one exact caller-supplied `CharacterBible` or `SceneBible` snapshot and its active V1
  AssetVersion;
- exact Promotion Maker identity/action records; and
- explicit `requested_at`.

It would not receive a Promotion Checker action, a final Decision field, a promotion-time Receipt or
`promotion_at`.

Decision finalization would additionally receive:

- the exact Promotion Request and every request-time value/byte needed to reconstruct it;
- the exact final generated current-status Subject, Request, Instruction, Decision, Evidence Record
  and historical Receipt;
- every final target Source Observation, complete predecessor/ancestor chain and reconciliation
  branch required by ADR-044;
- exact final Status Preparer and Checker identity/action records;
- one exact caller-supplied Character/Scene Bible snapshot and its active V1 AssetVersion at
  finalization;
- exact Promotion Checker identity/action records; and
- explicit `promotion_at`.

Every typed value would be exact-type revalidated. Every supplied formal document, retained record,
Prompt and PNG would be rehashed under its released codec or raw-byte rule. Every copied field would
be compared to its authoritative source. A digest string, ID, parsed Receipt, `CURRENT` literal,
database row, path or caller assertion alone is insufficient.

Each phase would verify the supplied primary AssetVersion Contract closure and its active Bible
snapshot binding.
It would not claim to have re-read or rehashed the imported primary media file unless those bytes
are separately and explicitly supplied under a future boundary.

## Explicit time and validity rules

All times would be explicit caller inputs in UTC with exact whole-second `Z` precision. No field may
come from a wall clock, filesystem timestamp, environment variable, database default or implicit
`now`.

Request preparation would require:

```text
requested_as_of == requested_at == maker_prepared_at
requested_as_of_status == CURRENT
```

It would freshly rebuild the complete ADR-044 status closure at `requested_as_of` and require the
exact request-time Receipt to have:

```text
receipt.as_of == requested_at
receipt.as_of_status == CURRENT
receipt.same_call_assessment_verified == true
receipt.present_currentness_asserted == false
```

The frozen Request deadline is uniquely defined as the earliest of:

- exactly 86,400 seconds after `requested_at`;
- `qualification_valid_until`;
- `manifest_valid_until`; and
- the request-time `status_valid_until`.

Decision finalization would require:

```text
requested_at <= promotion_at < request_valid_until
checker_reviewed_at == promotion_at
decision_at == promotion_at
promotion Receipt as_of == promotion_at
```

The complete ADR-044 closure would be replayed again in the same Decision-finalization call. A
positive Decision would require `as_of_status == CURRENT` at the exact `promotion_at`.

Every interval is half-open. Equality with `request_valid_until`, `qualification_valid_until`,
`manifest_valid_until` or either status `status_valid_until` is expired and cannot produce a positive
Decision. No grace period, deadline rounding, refresh, renewal or later human action extends an
existing value.

The Sidecar's `promotion_evidence_valid_until` would be the earliest of the positive Decision's
`qualification_valid_until`, `manifest_valid_until` and final `status_valid_until`. That value is a
historical horizon only. It is not a period during which consumers may skip fresh status replay.

## Primary AssetVersion binding

This Accepted decision defines one closed inline
`GeneratedReferencePromotionPrimaryAssetBindingV1` value:

```text
binding_profile=sdc.generated-reference-promotion-primary-asset-binding.v1
primary_asset_binding_sha256: LowerSha256
asset_purpose: CHARACTER_REFERENCE_ASSET | SCENE_REFERENCE_ASSET
subject_id: PortableId
asset_version_id: PortableId
legacy_asset_version_projection_sha256: LowerSha256
version: integer >= 1
content_sha256: LowerSha256
media_type=image/png
approval_ref: PortableId
provenance=IMPORTED_APPROVED_MEDIA
bible_active_asset_version_id: PortableId
```

The full binding digest would exclude only `primary_asset_binding_sha256`. The common inline builder
would require `asset_version_id == bible_active_asset_version_id`, the exact Character/Scene variant
implied by `asset_purpose`, exact subject ownership, and a fully revalidated supplied Bible and V1
AssetVersion closure. Request preparation would additionally require the
`requested_primary_asset_binding` to equal the Artifact, Outcome and Candidate expected active
AssetVersion ID/content SHA exactly. Decision finalization would instead admit a different but fully
valid final supplied active V1 binding only when its subject and purpose still equal the Request;
gate five would compare that `promotion_primary_asset_binding` with the requested binding. A
malformed binding or a subject/purpose change would remain a structural failure, while a valid
same-subject/purpose active-binding change would remain a reachable `FAIL` result.

The supplied exact V1 AssetVersion would still be fully revalidated. In addition, the Accepted
design derives `legacy_asset_version_projection_sha256` over the exact full released V1 identity
projection:

```text
Character: approval_ref, character_id, content_sha256, media_type, provenance,
           version, visual_description
Scene:     approval_ref, content_sha256, media_type, provenance, scene_id,
           version, visual_description
```

Those key sets and values are exactly the inputs used by the released V1 `derive_id` functions. The
new full digest would use its own domain plus the exact released legacy compact JSON codec:
UTF-8 without BOM, `sort_keys=true`, `ensure_ascii=false`, compact separators, no whitespace and no
terminal LF. It would preserve the exact supplied V1 strings and add no new NFC requirement. The
portable binding would not duplicate `visual_description`, but this independent full 64-hex digest
would bind it directly. The outer primary-binding digest would then bind that full legacy digest,
the exact revalidated V1 ID, subject, purpose, version, content, approval, provenance and active-
Bible relationship rather than depending on the 20-hex handle alone.

The Request would carry `requested_primary_asset_binding`. The Decision would carry that exact
Request value plus a separately rebuilt `promotion_primary_asset_binding` from the final supplied
Bible snapshot. A difference would be a valid negative Promotion gate result and would create no
Sidecar. A positive Sidecar would carry the equal final binding as `primary_asset_binding`.
Promotion would never change either Bible snapshot itself, and no future value would prove that a
supplied snapshot is repository-current or globally latest.

additional independence claim for packet preparation. Every supplied retained identity and action
record would be rehashed. The Promotion Maker and Promotion Checker action digests would have to be
different from one another and from every supplied upstream action digest, retained identity digest,
evidence digest, Prompt/PNG raw digest and formal semantic digest. An identity-record raw digest could
repeat only when byte-identical retained identity bytes are deliberately reused by roles whose tuple
equality the matrix permits; that permitted identity reuse is the sole exception to global digest
non-aliasing. Hash inequality alone would not prove distinct semantic identities: exact retained
human-reference bytes would be parsed and their `(identity_namespace, identity_ref)` tuples compared.

These rules prove only deterministic separation of supplied records. They do not authenticate a
person, employment, legal authority, competence or organizational approval.

## Fresh current-status replay requirement

The Request's historical Receipt would prove only that its exact Record and chain set were replayed
at `requested_at`. It would not authorize final Promotion.

ADR-044 validation layers in order:

1. exact Contract and raw retained-byte admission;
2. complete ancestor-closed chain replay;
3. exact Request target coverage;
4. joint Record replay;
5. record-as-of assessment at exact `promotion_at`; and
6. creation and verification of the exact historical Receipt for that call.

The finalization closure could use the same exact ADR-044 Evidence Record as the Request or one new
append-only Record containing later observations. In either case, request-time and final status
values would have to bind the same exact Status Subject Closure: the same Candidate, Qualification
Decision, Manifest, subject and asset purpose.

a byte-identical monotonic superset of the request-time set. Every request-time target anchor and
every earlier branch would have to be covered in one of exactly two ways: the anchor remains a final
target, or its exact Observation becomes a complete ancestor of an explicitly supplied final
successor/reconciliation target that closes every retained predecessor branch under ADR-044. A
later successor could therefore replace an older target's current-target role without deleting the
older Observation from the final ancestor closure. The final Record would derive its own canonical
zero-based target ordinals; ordinal changes alone would not imply target mutation.

earlier occurrence, predecessor or reconciliation branch could be omitted, substituted or
rewritten. The final call would rebuild full ancestor closure, target coverage and joint replay
across that entire monotonic closure. It could not replace the Candidate, Qualification, Manifest,
policy or Status Subject. The Promotion Checker action would bind the exact final Receipt. No
operation would discover or select a latest, best or favorable Record from storage.

cycle, malformed fork, incomplete coverage, replay mismatch or omitted target would be an operation
failure. A structurally complete current-status result of `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`, `HELD` or

Promotion Checker.

- no path, URL, credential, Provider task ID, account identifier, response body, raw legal document
  or unbounded private material in a portable Contract.

Upstream ADR-042/043/044 formal documents, retained records, Prompt bytes and status-chain inputs
would retain their own released exact resource limits; this Accepted decision does not widen or
narrow them.

Formal documents would bind privacy-minimized references and digests only. They would neither
authorize retention nor authenticate the retained material. A future operation would accept exact
byte buffers. Any optional local file adapter would require its own explicit regular-file,
symlink/ReparsePoint, hard-link, size-drift and TOCTOU controls.

## Failure behavior

Structural construction and verification would use these frozen stable umbrella codes in exact
priority order:

```text
EXACT_INPUT_TYPE_REQUIRED
DOCUMENT_RESOURCE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED
CANONICAL_JSON_REQUIRED
CONTRACT_FIELD_INVALID
POLICY_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
SEMANTIC_ID_OR_DIGEST_MISMATCH
UPSTREAM_CLOSURE_MISMATCH
PRIMARY_ASSET_BINDING_CLOSURE_INVALID
TIME_WINDOW_INVALID_OR_EXPIRED
ROLE_SEPARATION_VIOLATION
STATUS_REPLAY_FAILED
PROMOTION_GATE_NOT_PASS
AUTHORITY_SURFACE_NONZERO
PROHIBITED_BOUNDARY_CONNECTION
```

Specific ADR-042/043/044 nested errors would be preserved before the next outer code. No wrapper
would parse exception text or replace a more specific nested code with a later generic code.

Structural or replay failure during Request preparation would produce no Request. The same class of
failure during Decision finalization would produce no Decision or Sidecar and would not mutate the
already existing Request. This includes malformed types/bytes, missing predecessor, incomplete
ancestor closure, cycle, malformed fork, coverage failure, policy drift, digest mismatch, role
alias, invalid time, unauthorized field, prohibited connection, primary-binding closure mismatch or
omission/substitution of a retained request-time Observation or branch from a new final Record.

`PRIMARY_ASSET_BINDING_CLOSURE_INVALID` would mean that a supplied Bible, AssetVersion or inline
binding cannot be exactly reconstructed. It would produce no formal output. A valid final Bible
snapshot with an exactly reconstructed but different active binding would instead use the
`PRIMARY_BINDING_NO_LONGER_ACTIVE` negative gate issue.

After one valid Request exists, complete final replay may produce a valid negative Decision for:

- a structurally valid `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`, `HELD` or `INDETERMINATE` final status;
- a valid final caller-supplied Bible snapshot whose active primary binding no longer equals the
  Request binding;
- a Promotion human gate recorded as `FAIL` or `INDETERMINATE`.

No negative Decision would create a Sidecar. A positive finalizer would return the Decision and
Sidecar together from one pure in-memory call. If either value cannot be constructed and reverified,
the call would return neither. Persistence atomicity remains outside this boundary.

`PROMOTION_GATE_NOT_PASS` would apply only when a caller attempts to construct or verify a Sidecar
from a non-positive Decision. A normal valid `FAIL` or `INDETERMINATE` finalization would return its
Decision and would not raise that umbrella error.

No operation may repair, coerce, waive, select an alternate value, refresh a deadline, retry a
Provider operation or create a partial output.

## Permitted claims and explicit non-proofs

Only after explicitly authorized implementation, first-party synthetic known-answer review and
merge could SDC claim that:

- one exact Promotion Request closed one exact Candidate, Qualification, Manifest, request-time
  current-status and primary AssetVersion binding packet;
- one distinct Promotion Checker recorded one deterministic Decision after fresh replay at exact
  `promotion_at`;
- one positive Decision produced one immutable post-promotion Sidecar without changing the primary
  AssetVersion or Compiler output; and
- all new values remained offline and granted zero Provider, Runtime, asset-use, publication or
  execution authority.

Even then, SDC could not claim that:

- the generated bytes became a current AssetVersion or active Bible binding;
- a Sidecar proves ownership, license, commercial use or global real-world currentness;
- a retained identity reference authenticates a real person or authority;
- absence of supplied adverse evidence proves that none exists;
- the Sidecar remains current after `promotion_at`;
- a historical evidence horizon allows a consumer to skip fresh replay;
- the PNG contains any Profile role, Prompt constraint or QC expectation;
- Provider compatibility proves capability, availability, entitlement or authorization;
- the Sidecar is role-bound, Provider-input eligible or executable;
- Runtime, Retry, network, credentials, payment, publication, retention or training is permitted;
  or
- equal bytes from another Candidate occurrence inherit the Promotion closure.

## Consequences

Positive consequences of the Accepted decision, if separately implemented:

- generated media would gain a truthful post-promotion representation without corrupting imported V1
  provenance;
- the existing primary AssetVersion and every existing Compiler identity would remain unchanged;
- full Qualification, Rights and current-status evidence would have to be replayed rather than trusted by
  digest alone;
- Promotion would obtain its own independent human decision and role separation;
- Request, Decision and Sidecar identities would remain acyclic and deterministic;
- exact Rights scope could not be silently expanded or renewed;
- later adverse evidence would remain append-only and could not rewrite history;
- multi-reference Provider input would remain a visibly separate future decision; and
- the Schema Registry would remain append-only from 83 to 86 in a future authorized BUILD.

Costs and limitations:

- Request and Decision each require a complete current-status replay;
- the Promotion Checker would have to be independent of the Qualifier, Manifest Checker,
  request-time Status Checker and final Status Checker;
- an approved Sidecar would still not enter the current Compiler or Provider input;
- renewed Qualification or Manifest review would require a new Promotion closure;
- no current/latest Sidecar resolver would exist;
- one composite image remains unsplit and unassigned to roles;
- three new Schemas and extensive known-answer/negative tests would be required; and
- active generated assets would still require a later versioned AssetVersion/Bible/Compiler ADR.
